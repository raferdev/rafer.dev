This folder sets `ordering: timestamp`, which sorts by the `date` in each
post's frontmatter rather than by filename.

## Why frontmatter and not the file's mtime

Git does not preserve modification times. A fresh clone rewrites every mtime to
checkout time, so filesystem timestamps would reorder the blog for each person
who cloned it. The declared `date` is stable.
