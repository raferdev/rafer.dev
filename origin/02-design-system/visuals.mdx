## Themes

Both themes resolve from one token set. Never reach for a `dark:` override.

A component that uses `bg-card text-card-foreground` is already correct in both
themes. Writing `bg-white dark:bg-zinc-900` is the same idea expressed twice,
and the two copies drift apart the moment a token changes.

## What flips and what does not

Hue stays fixed across themes. What changes is lightness, and which end of the
ramp a role points at:

| Role           | Light      | Dark                    |
| -------------- | ---------- | ----------------------- |
| `--primary`    | brand-700  | brand-400               |
| `--background` | white      | brand-tinted near-black |
| `--border`     | solid tint | white at 12%            |

Borders in dark mode are a translucent white rather than a fixed colour, so
they hold up over both the background and the raised card surface without a
second token.

## The wordmark

The logo follows the same rule. Its letterforms are `currentColor`, so they
take the foreground of whatever sits behind them, and its accent marks read
`var(--brand-700)`. One set of paths, no light and dark variants, and the blue
cannot drift away from the palette because it _is_ the palette.
